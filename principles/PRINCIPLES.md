# Principles

These should change when better evidence appears.

## Understanding > memorizing
Knowing the name of a pattern matters less than understanding the problem that caused it to exist.

## Build to test understanding
If I think I understand a concept, a small implementation should expose weak parts of my mental model.

## Architecture follows constraints
There is no universally best architecture. Decisions should follow domain, team, scale, reliability, operational, and change constraints.

## Prefer simple systems until complexity earns its place
Complexity should solve a real problem, not signal sophistication.

## AI should amplify my thinking, not replace understanding
AI can increase how much I can build, but I should remain capable of reasoning about important architecture, data, security, and product decisions.

## Interesting does not automatically mean new project
Capture first. Experiment second. Promote only when the idea remains valuable after novelty fades.

## Evidence > self-image
Skill claims should connect to things I can explain, build, debug, review, or teach.

## Changing my mind is progress
When better evidence changes a belief, record the change instead of defending the old position.

## Curiosity needs focus
A backlog protects curiosity. A small current-focus list protects depth.
