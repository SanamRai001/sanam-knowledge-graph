# Code of Conduct

Sanam Knowledge Graph is intended to be a welcoming place for people who want to learn, reflect, and improve how they understand things.

## Expected behavior

When participating in issues, discussions, reviews, or pull requests:

- be respectful and constructive
- critique ideas rather than people
- assume good intent while still addressing harmful behavior
- make room for beginners and different learning styles
- respect privacy and boundaries
- give credit when building on someone else's work
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks

## Privacy matters here

Knowledge graphs can contain personal reflections.

Do not pressure contributors to reveal private information, publish personal branches, justify sensitive life choices, or share information they are not comfortable making public.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include:

- harassment or targeted insults
- discriminatory or demeaning comments
- publishing someone else's private information
- threats or intimidation
- repeated unwanted contact
- deliberate disruption of project collaboration

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations.

Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.

## Scope

This applies to participation in the project's official GitHub spaces and when representing the project in other public spaces.
