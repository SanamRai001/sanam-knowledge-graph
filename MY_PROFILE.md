# Sanam's Knowledge Profile

This branch is my living knowledge graph.

It tracks what I can currently do, what I am learning, where my understanding is weak, what questions keep pulling me forward, and what I build to test that understanding.

## Direction

I want to become the kind of builder who can enter an unfamiliar technical or creative area, understand its underlying principles, connect those ideas to things I already know, and turn those connections into useful systems and products.

## Core loop

**Learn → Understand → Connect → Build → Reflect → Evolve**

## Rules

- Be honest about skill depth.
- Prefer evidence from real work over self-assessment.
- Record gaps explicitly.
- Keep current learning focus small.
- Capture ideas without turning all of them into projects.
- Revisit old beliefs when new evidence appears.
