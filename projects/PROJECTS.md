# Projects

Projects are evidence of applied knowledge.

## Restaurant Management System (RMS)

**Purpose:** Build a production-oriented restaurant operating system spanning administration, POS, KOT/kitchen workflows, payments, tenancy, and platform management.

**Concepts demonstrated:**
- Backend domain modeling
- Multi-tenancy
- Authentication and RBAC
- Database-backed workflows
- Order state machines
- POS / KOT flows
- Business rules
- Modular architecture
- Testing and audits

**Learning value:** A long-lived system where changing requirements expose the consequences of architecture decisions.
